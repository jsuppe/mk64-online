V76 · AUTOMATED CPU RACERS

Four peers. Work to do.

Mario Raceway, captured October 13. The simulations matched, but repeated wall contact, idle driving, and unusual camera behavior have been reported. This is not a normal-player-camera demonstration.

What does “the simulations matched” mean?

In one v76 four-instance Mario Raceway run, 3,732 shared logical frames were compared without divergence. That is synchronization evidence for that run—not proof of good driving, correct cameras, complete races, or every course.

Race automation uses native CPU participation; it is not a recording of four human players. Read the driving and camera qualification plan.

DEVELOPER PREVIEW

Interested in trying it?

You’ll need a compatible NetPak64-enabled emulator, a matching modified game build, and an accessible relay. A standard emulator alone is not enough.

The game source requires assets extracted from your own lawful copy. This site does not provide ROMs or Nintendo assets.

STILL EXPERIMENTAL

The player experience

  • Four-peer camera behavior needs correction
  • Complete-race and all-course qualification is unfinished
  • Physical-console networking is not verified end-to-end
